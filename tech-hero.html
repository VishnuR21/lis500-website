Our Tech Heroes

Each of us chose a different tech hero and connected their work to equity, access, and intersectionality in tech.

Vishnu's Tech Hero: Angela Siefer

Angela Siefer is a national leader in digital inclusion and has spent years working to close gaps in broadband access, affordable devices, and digital skills. Her work focuses on the idea that internet access is a basic need for education, jobs, healthcare, and civic participation.

As Executive Director of the National Digital Inclusion Alliance (NDIA), she supports local organizations that serve communities most affected by digital inequality. Her leadership reflects intersectionality because internet barriers are often connected to race, income, geography, age, and disability all at once.

  • National Digital Inclusion Alliance (NDIA) Organization leading work on digital equity and internet access.
  • NDIA definitions: digital equity and digital inclusion Clear definitions used by practitioners and community programs.

Kavya’s Tech Hero: Angie Jones

Angie Jones is the Vice President of Engineering, AI Tools & Enablement at Block, Inc. She is an award‑winning educator, an international keynote speaker, and a Master Inventor with 27 patented inventions. She also teaches and advocates for better pathways into tech education and testing.

Her work reflects intersectionality by pushing for broader access to learning and by mentoring new technologists. She has highlighted the importance of bringing more women and minorities into tech and has supported programs like Black Girls Code.
